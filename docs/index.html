Built to get out of your way

A quiet reader for your local comic collection, on Mac and PC.

Three reading modes

Single page, double-page spread, or vertical scroll for webtoons. Read left to right or right to left.

Folders, CBZ/ZIP, CBR/RAR

Open folders or archives, including nested archives up to three levels deep. Pages follow natural filename order.

Keyboard-first

Turn pages, jump to a page, bookmark a favorite scene, and change layouts from the keyboard. See shortcuts for each platform below.

Smooth reading

Zoom, fit to screen, width or height, and browse page thumbnails without leaving the reader.

Bookmarks & favorites

Save a page bookmark or star a book for quick access from the library.

Resume where you left off

Each app remembers your reading position and per-series reading settings on that device.

Open from your file manager

On macOS, use Finder’s Open With menu. On Windows, add the optional “Open with Panely” entry in Settings.

Read comics in a language you don’t know

Experimental on-device translation reads speech balloons and places a machine translation over the page. Both apps let you correct balloons and export translated books; quality and available features vary by platform.

macOS

Requires macOS 26 or later. Uses Apple’s on-device text recognition and Translation, with system language packs.

macOS translation guide →

Windows

Download the language pack once, then translate offline. Uses local OCR and M2M100 models. Translation is experimental; short lines and idioms can be inaccurate. Use Fast mode; Accurate mode is untested.

Install & open Panely

Choose the instructions for your platform. Both apps run without administrator installation.

macOS

  1. Download and unzip the macOS release. Move Panely.app into Applications.
  2. Panely is not notarized by Apple. If Gatekeeper blocks it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose Open Anyway beside the Panely notice.
  3. Confirm the system prompt, then open a folder or comic archive in Panely.
Terminal alternative (after moving the app to Applications) 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Panely.app

Windows

  1. Choose x64 for most Intel / AMD PCs, or ARM64 for Windows on ARM. Extract the ZIP to a folder you keep.
  2. Run Panely.exe. No installer, administrator rights, or separate .NET runtime is needed.
  3. The app is not code-signed. If SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC,” choose More info → Run anyway.

To update, close Panely and replace the folder’s contents with the new ZIP. Checksums are in SHA256SUMS.txt on the release page.
